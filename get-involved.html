Common questions

The questions donors actually ask.

Will I receive a tax receipt for my donation?
Yes — CanadaHelps issues a CRA-compliant tax receipt automatically and emails it to you within minutes. Fondation LoLa is a registered Canadian charity (BN 758770812RR0001).
What percentage of my donation actually goes to the library?
100% of donations go to construction and books. The founding family covers all administrative and operating costs personally. CanadaHelps charges a small platform fee (around 3.75%) for processing and tax receipt issuance — that's the only deduction before funds reach us.
Why is construction paused?
We launched an Indiegogo campaign in late 2024 with a goal of $83,500 and raised $8,847. After the campaign closed, we learned that most foundations and major donors prefer to fund programs rather than construction. Rather than continue without a clear path forward, the family decided to pause and self-fund the build at our own pace. Slower, but more certain.
When will the library open?
Our current target is 2027. The original target of December 2025 was set during the Indiegogo campaign and has slipped due to the funding pause. We will not commit to a date until we are confident we can hold it.
Can I set up a recurring monthly donation?
Yes — CanadaHelps supports monthly giving. On the donation page, choose "Monthly" instead of "One-time." Monthly donors are our most stable funding source and we are deeply grateful for them.
Can I donate books or equipment?
Yes. We accept books in French, Lingala, Kikongo, and English (children's, young adult, reference, study). For equipment donations or international shipments, please email info@lolafondation.com first so we can confirm logistics.
Is my donation refundable?
Donations are generally non-refundable as tax receipts are issued at the time of donation. If you donated in error, contact us within 7 days at info@lolafondation.com and we'll work with you and CanadaHelps to resolve it.
How can I follow the project's progress?
Subscribe to our quarterly updates from the Progress page. We post photos from the build, donor receipts, and what worked vs. what didn't. We also publish updates on Facebook, Twitter/X, YouTube, and LinkedIn.
